🗺️ Sectional chart symbology

A quick visual key for reading a VFR sectional. The color rule does most of the work: blue marks towered airports and Class B/C/D-with-tower features, while magenta marks non-towered airports, Class C, and Class E that starts at the surface. This is a study aid; the real knowledge test shows excerpts from the FAA testing supplement.

Airports
Towered airportBlue. Has an operating control tower.
Non-towered airportMagenta. No control tower.
Fuel / servicesTick marks around the circle.
Airspace boundaries
Class BSolid blue line.
Class CSolid magenta line.
Class DDashed blue line.
Class E to surfaceDashed magenta line.
Class E at 700 ft AGLSoft magenta fade (faded side = lower floor).
Class E at 1,200 ft AGLSoft blue fade.
Prohibited / restrictedBlue line with hatching inside.
MOA / alert areaMagenta line with hatching.
Altitude labels
Ceiling over floor118/40 = top 11,800, floor 4,000 ft MSL.
Up to surfaceSFC = from the ground up.
Magenta floor figureClass E floor in feet AGL.
Obstacles and terrain
Single obstacleTower, antenna, or similar.
Group obstacleTwo or more close together.
Lighted obstacleMagenta rays = lighting.
Obstacle heightTop MSL, and (AGL) above ground.
Max elevation figure2 5 = 2,500 ft, highest in that quadrant.
Navigation
Visual checkpointMagenta flag at a landmark.
Victor airwayLow-altitude route; expect traffic.
